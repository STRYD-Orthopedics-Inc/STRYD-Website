Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026 · Version 2026-10-06

STRYD Orthopedics, Inc. (“STRYD”, “we”) is building a wearable for knee health. This policy explains what we collect on strydorthopedics.com, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to match what the site actually does today.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist

  • Email address, to send your confirmation link and updates.
  • Who you are (one of: parent of a travel or high school athlete, runner or active adult, college or pro athlete, other), your sport (optional, free text), and where you heard about us.
  • Your consent record: that you confirmed you are 18 or older and agreed to this policy and to email updates, the time you did so, and the version of this policy at that time.
  • A first-name guess we derive from your email address (for example “jordan.smith@…” becomes “Jordan”), used only to personalize a greeting. We do not ask for your name. If we can't make a confident guess we use no name.
  • Whether you confirmed your email, and when. Only confirmed signups count as being on the waitlist.

If you accept analytics cookies

Only after you click “Accept” on the cookie banner, we:

  • Remember how you arrived: campaign tags in the link you clicked (UTM parameters), a tracked-link code if you used one of our short links, the page you landed on (path only), and the website you came from (host name only). We keep this in your browser for up to 30 days and attach it to your signup if you join.
  • Use PostHog to measure which pages are viewed, what is clicked, how far videos are watched, and where people get stuck on the form. This includes session replays with every text box and form input masked, so what you type is not recorded. We do not send your email address to PostHog.

If you click “Decline”, none of the above is loaded or stored, and the form still works. You can change your choice any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

Always, regardless of your choice

  • A salted hash of your IP address to limit repeated submissions, kept for about two hours. We do not store your raw IP address ourselves. Our hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs.
  • Click counts on our short links, with no personal information (no IP address, no browser details), and with obvious bots flagged.
  • Settings stored in your browser for the site to work: your cookie choice and your light/dark theme choice.

If you use the contact form

The “Get in Touch” form on the home page collects the details you type (name, email, phone, organization, role, message). It is handled by Netlify Forms and is used only to reply to you.

How long we keep it

We keep your waitlist information until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may periodically delete signups that were never confirmed. Aggregate counts that no longer identify you may be kept.

Your choices and rights

You can unsubscribe using the link in any email we send. To access, correct or delete your information, email contact@strydorthopedics.com and we will respond. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under local law.

Age

The waitlist is for adults. You must be 18 or older to sign up. Parents sign up for themselves and must not enter a child's information. This site is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

STRYD is a wellness product. Nothing on this site is medical advice.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date and version above. If a change affects how we use your information, we will ask for your agreement again where the law requires it.
