FAQ

Common Questions

What is STRYD?

STRYD is a wearable knee sleeve and companion app that helps you understand how your body is responding to training.

Is STRYD a medical device?

No. STRYD is a wellness product, not a medical device, and it isn't a substitute for professional medical advice.

Who is it for?

Athletes and active adults — and the parents of young athletes — who want a clearer view of load and strain over time.

What happens when I join the waitlist?
