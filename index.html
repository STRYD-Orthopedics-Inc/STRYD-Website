FAQ

Common Questions

What is STRYD?

STRYD is a wearable knee sleeve and companion app that helps you understand how your body is responding to training.

How does STRYD work?

You wear the sleeve while you train. Its sensors pick up how your legs are working, and the app turns that into trends you can read at a glance.

Is STRYD a medical device?

No. STRYD is a wellness product, not a medical device, and it isn't a substitute for professional medical advice.

Who is it for?

Parents of travel and high school athletes, runners and active adults, and college and pro athletes and teams who want a clearer view of load and strain over time.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You'll hear from us with product updates and early-access information. We won't spam you.

Where is STRYD Orthopedics based?

STRYD Orthopedics, Inc. is based in Birmingham, Alabama, in the United States.

Is STRYD Orthopedics related to other companies with similar names?

No. STRYD Orthopedics, Inc. is an independent company, and its patent-pending wearable knee sleeve is its own product. We aren't affiliated with other companies that happen to use similar names.
